The weight of the block of items [i, j), from the prefix sums of their weights.

Every comparison of block weights goes through this one computation, so that its rounding is the same wherever a block is weighed. It is non-decreasing in j and non-increasing in i, since the prefix sums are non-decreasing and rounding is monotone.

std::size_t max_parts )

The least weight of the heaviest block of any cut into at most max_parts blocks.

Non-negative doubles are ordered as their bit patterns are, so a binary search over the bit patterns from 0 to the total weight, which always admits a single block, finds the least bound that admits a cut exactly, in at most 64 steps.
