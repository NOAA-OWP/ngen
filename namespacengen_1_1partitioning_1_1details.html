std::vector< CatchmentNexusLink > ngen::partitioning::details::catchment_nexus_links ( const geojson::GeoJSON & catchments)

Read the nexus each catchment in catchments drains to.

Takes catchments as any hydrofabric reader presents them, with the downstream nexus under toid. A catchment with no toid drains to no nexus.

◆ group_by_nexus()

NexusContributors ngen::partitioning::details::group_by_nexus ( const std::vector< CatchmentNexusLink > & links)

Group links by nexus.

Nexuses keep the order in which they first appear in links, so that a hydrofabric whose features are stored in network order yields parts made of neighboring nexuses. Each catchment that drains to no nexus becomes a group of its own, in its place in that order.
