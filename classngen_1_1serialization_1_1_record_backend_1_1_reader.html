Detailed Description

A read handle for one restore session.

Scoped at construction by an IdPredicate. Obtained only via RecordBackend::reader(); cannot be constructed standalone or mixed across backends. "Not found" is returned on the expected<> error arm as BackendError::Kind::NotFound.
